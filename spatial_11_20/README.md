# Spatial 1–25 game

This oTree app is the first app in the `11_20` session sequence configured in
`settings.py`.

The game runs 10 rounds with two participants per group. Pairings are randomized
each round. Within each pair, one player is assigned the `opponent number - 1`
target and the other the `opponent number - 5` target. The number board is 5 × 5
and covers 1–25.

One round from each of rounds 1–3, 4–7, and 8–10 is randomly selected as an
extra-reasoning round. In those rounds, participants may keep or revise their
number and must provide a reason. Each player's round payoff is their final
chosen number plus a 10-unit bonus if they meet their target. Results are shown
after all participants submit and display the round history, target success,
the final payoff including any bonus, and cumulative payoff.
